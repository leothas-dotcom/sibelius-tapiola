## Otsikko

I asked Claude Fable 5.2 to make a video for Sibelius. It is INSANE.

## Kuvaus

Sound goes in. A forest comes out.

I play in an orchestra. I gave Claude Fable 5.2 a recording of Sibelius' Tapiola and asked it to make a video where you can see what the orchestra is doing. This is what came out.

Nothing here is AI-generated video. Claude listened to the recording, read the score, and then wrote a program that draws every frame from the music itself. When the strings get louder, the wind picks up. When the violas play, snow starts to fall. When the brass comes in, a red sun rises that gives no warmth.

How it works, briefly:

– Claude measured the recording: where each phrase starts, how loud each group of instruments is, where the accents land.
– It read the score to know who is playing, so the picture follows the instrument that is speaking and not just the volume.
– Every instrument became a force of nature. Loudness is a push, like wind: things start moving slowly and keep drifting after the sound has stopped.
– I watched every draft and told it what I heard and what was missing. About two hundred notes later, this is the result.

Tapiola (1926) is the last big orchestral piece Sibelius wrote: eighteen minutes inside the forest of Tapio, the forest god of Finnish myth.

When I was a child, my father and I listened to records together, and he told me what was happening in the music: who speaks, who arrives. I wanted a film that does the same thing for you.
